# Sanctuary NPC portrait prompts

Five faces at THE LAST SANCTUARY (camp screen column + talk window). Save each result as
`assets/npcs/<id>.webp` (or .png / .jpg): `maren`, `ansel`, `ysolde`, `ferryman`, `seer`.
The loader (src/render/NpcArt.js) crops to 76x90, centred, biased toward the top, so the face
must be large and in the upper half. No green screen: these are full paintings with a background.

Shared style (append to every prompt):

> Dark fantasy oil painting, grimdark biblical apocalypse, painterly brushwork like the portraits of the Four Horsemen, close head-and-shoulders portrait, face fills the upper half of the frame, looking at the viewer, warm candle and campfire light from one side, deep shadow, muted desaturated palette with one accent colour, vertical 4:5 composition, no text, no border, no watermark.

## maren - Elder Maren, Keeper of the Seals
> Portrait of a very old man, keeper of seven broken seals, deeply lined weathered face, long white beard, tired wise grey eyes, worn brown wool hood and robe with frayed edges, a cord of seven wax seal discs (three of them cracked) around his neck, ancient parchment and candle smoke behind him, ochre and umber palette.

## ansel - Brother Ansel, War Chaplain
> Portrait of a battle-worn war priest in his fifties, shaved head with a small gold sun-cross mark painted on his forehead, short dark beard streaked with grey, a scar across one cheek, blood-red cassock over dented chainmail, a prayer rope wrapped around his fist, faint banners of a lost army behind him, crimson and dark iron palette.

## ysolde - Ysolde, Herbalist of the Last Field
> Portrait of a stubborn woman in her thirties, freckled sun-browned face, sharp green eyes, loose auburn hair under a moss-green hood, dried herbs and a sprig of living green leaves tucked into her hood, smudges of soil on her cheek, faint green glow of a single living plant against a blighted dead field behind her, olive and moss green palette.

## ferryman - The Ferryman, Guide at the Pale Threshold
> Portrait of an ageless gaunt ferryman of the dead, pale grey-blue skin, hollow cheeks, softly glowing pale cyan eyes, a deep slate-blue hood with water-stained edges, an old coin on a cord, a dim lantern glow, black river mist and faint drifting souls behind him, cold blue and silver palette.

## seer - The Blind Seer, She Who Read the Last Page
> Portrait of an old prophetess who put out her own eyes, a white linen blindfold across her eyes with faint stains beneath, serene sorrowful face, long silver hair, a thin tarnished gold circlet crown, deep violet robes, a burned page of a holy book held near her chin, violet candlelight and drifting ash behind her, purple and old gold palette.
